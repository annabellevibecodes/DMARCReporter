Data Enrichment

Resolves reverse DNS and WHOIS data for all source IPs, checks DMARC, BIMI, and MTA-STS DNS records for all known domains, and expands each domain's SPF record into the networks it authorises (following include / redirect / a / mx, capped at 10 DNS lookups). Results are cached and used across the dashboard; the SPF expansion powers the spoof-volume split and the spoof-spike alert.
